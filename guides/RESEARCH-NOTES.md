# Reading notes: organization that is easier to use

These notes support a draft workflow refinement. They do not establish that DOX improves ADHD symptoms or that the authors endorse it. The hierarchy, original scope contracts and evidence distinctions remain binding.

## What was read

- **Ellie Middleton, published planning/prioritization extract (2024):** the author discusses clearer instructions, help sequencing tasks, adapting priorities to circumstances and working on one step at a time. This is practical author advice, not a controlled trial. [Read the edited book extract](https://www.stylist.co.uk/health/mental-health/adhd-autism-planning-prioritisation/916970).
- **Middleton, How to Be You, publisher outline:** describes a launch pad for essentials and visual idea parking among the book's suggestions. The outline was read; the complete book was not available in this review. [Publisher description](https://www.penguin.co.uk/books/462873/how-to-be-you-by-middleton-ellie/9781405971683).
- **Ana-Maria Butura, doctoral thesis, official abstract (2025):** describes internal experiences, context-dependent presentation and the value of first-person reports in work on girls and women with ADHD. This review read the detailed abstract, not every thesis chapter. It is not a documentation intervention study. [King's College London thesis record](https://kclpure.kcl.ac.uk/portal/en/studentTheses/adhd-in-girls-and-women-the-significance-of-subjective-perception/).
- **Butura-Santos, Pavlopoulou and Sonuga-Barke, 2026 accepted editorial:** distinguishes support from effortful concealment/compensation and cautions that ADHD masking remains insufficiently defined and studied. Visible functioning can leave important effort unobserved. The editorial was read; it is conceptual argument, not an experiment demonstrating an organization method. [Read the accepted manuscript](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10231560/3/Pavlopoulou_AMBS_Editorial_BJP_final_clean.pdf).

## DOX adaptations to test

The rules below are our design choices informed by that reading, not instructions authored or validated by those writers.

| Problem | Draft adaptation | What would count as useful |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A next action names a project but leaves its starting point unclear | Put the exact file/page/command in the existing next-action or working-details field | The person can locate the start without searching chat history |
| The owner has to choose between an entire backlog of possibilities | The agent sequences one step and gives a short real reason for priority when needed | The person understands the next move without planning the whole project |
| A new thought displaces current work | Preserve it in the existing parked inbox without a date or commitment | The thought remains retrievable while the current action remains clear |
| Returning after a lapse requires catching up on the whole system | Reconcile state, show the short view and offer a small authorized action | The person can resume before clearing old notes |
| Documents look correct but the person says they are too much to use | Treat the reported friction as a separate usability issue | A concrete adjustment helps finding, starting or resuming |
| A text explanation is not useful to its reader | Offer a small visual map derived from the same state | The format aids understanding without creating another editable authority |

The last two adaptations also depend on the reader's expressed preferences. Do not infer universal needs from gender, diagnosis, appearance or one successful session.

## What is deliberately excluded

- No diagnosis or measurement of masking, exhaustion or executive function.
- No energy scores, mandatory daily check-in, fixed day schedule or extra personal-maintenance file.
- No claim that the findings in girls and women automatically generalize to every user.
- No requirement to remove the instruction hierarchy, drop verification or relax permissions to make a step feel easier.
- No promise of automatic maintenance when an agent is inactive.

## How to review the draft

Use the fictional scenarios in EXAMPLE.md and the existing starter. Verify that exact starting points, one current action, parked ideas, user-reported friction and mandatory hierarchy maintenance can coexist. Actual benefit still requires user feedback in real work; passing link and document checks does not establish it.
