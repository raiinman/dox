# Design decisions

## Accepted: returning is the main task

Accurate agent instructions can still leave a person unable to tell what happened last. The human entry point answers where work stopped and what happens next before showing technical detail. One next action reduces the decisions required to resume. Real-use benefit remains unverified.

## Accepted: agents carry the maintenance

The owner can capture rough text without tags or priorities. Agents reconcile state and preserve choices. No daily ritual or backlog-clearing requirement. Checkpoints during work reduce dependence on a perfect final handoff, but abrupt interruptions still need inspection. This is a workflow for active agents, not a background service.

## Accepted: one state authority with copy identity

Working source, installed app and published repository may differ. Results name their scope and identity. Existing status systems can remain authoritative; adoption maps them rather than duplicating them. This trades universal filenames for fewer competing records.

## Accepted: preserve detail without putting it first

AGENTS holds rules, DECISIONS holds choices, INBOX holds capture, and the resume page links to existing specs and evidence. Failures and approval boundaries survive summarization. Prior state and superseded choices remain recoverable. More folders do not automatically justify more instruction files.

## Accepted: keep the portable framework impersonal

Examples are fictional. Personal research informs design without being included in a public kit. No private paths or chat exports belong in reusable product documentation.

## Accepted 2026-11-16: restore hierarchy maintenance alongside the resume workflow

The owner chose to retain the resume page, checkpoints, inbox, decisions and evidence distinctions, and restore explicit scope ownership, child indexes, standard child-document structure and mandatory hierarchy maintenance.

This supersedes the first redesign's omission of required child indexes and its less explicit hierarchy maintenance. Existing earlier design reasoning remains above; it does not exempt agents from the restored contract. The four root starter files are an entry kit. Real project boundaries receive child instructions, each indexed by its direct parent. The human resume page remains short and singular.

## Validation still needed

This is not clinically validated and does not guarantee adherence or synchronization. Its first real-use check is whether the owner can return after an interruption and use the short page to identify where work stopped and what happens next. Improve that experience before adding more documentation.
